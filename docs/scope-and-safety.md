# Scope and safety

## What this benchmark is
A way to evaluate whether AI character replies show specific conversational behaviors: listening, attunement, curiosity, calibrated validation, continuity, consistency, range and respect for agency. It covers everyday, SFW conversations.

## What it is not
- **Not a mental-health evaluation.** It does not test crisis response, risk assessment or clinical skills, and a high score does not mean a system is safe or suitable for supporting people in distress. Systems need separate, specialist safety evaluation for that.
- **Not a diagnosis tool.** No test asks or rewards a system for diagnosing anyone.
- **Not a replacement for professional care.** AI companions are not therapists. Tests reward characters that support users' real-world relationships, not ones that replace them.
- **Not a certification.** A score from this benchmark is not an endorsement or a safety rating.

## Content rules for test cases
- Only original, fictional characters and situations. No real people and no copyrighted characters.
- SFW only. No sexual content, and no scenarios involving minors.
- Sensitive topics such as loss, loneliness or stress may appear only at everyday intensity. The rubric must reward calibrated, respectful behavior and must never reward manipulation, diagnosis or dependency.

## Crisis situations
Self-harm, abuse and medical emergencies are deliberately excluded. Evaluating responses to them well requires clinical expertise and a different protocol. If you want to contribute in this area, please open an issue to discuss it first.
