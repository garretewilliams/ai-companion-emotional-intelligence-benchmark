# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), as its code of conduct. The summary below is for convenience; the linked text is authoritative.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

* Be respectful and constructive, especially when critiquing methodology or results.
* Assume good faith. People evaluating competing systems are welcome here.
* Give and accept feedback gracefully.
* Focus on what is best for the benchmark's usefulness and credibility.

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
* Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
* Publishing others' private information without permission.
* Deliberately misrepresenting results, or submitting fabricated data.
* Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere.

Report problems privately to the maintainers by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/garretewilliams/ai-character-memory-benchmark/security/advisories/new) (which is visible only to maintainers) or by contacting the maintainer through the details on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, in line with the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
